# Texture attribution

Surface maps used by the solar-system renderer. These are **observed imagery**, not procedural
art — which is why they replaced the generated surfaces that shipped first. A rendering of Earth
with invented continents is a fabrication; a real map is data, and data gets a source.

## What these maps do and do not claim

They are surface *appearance* only. Every quantity the application computes — position, orbit,
distance, illumination direction, axial tilt, rotation rate — comes from the science service and
carries its own provenance. A texture never contributes to a number.

Cloud patterns in particular are a single fixed snapshot, not current weather. The panel says so.
